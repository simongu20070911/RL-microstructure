## 1. Binance Rebate Analysis

### 1.1 VIP Level Structure (2025)

**Standard Trading Fees (VIP 0):**
- Spot Trading: 0.10% maker/taker fees
- 25% reduction with BNB payment (0.075% effective rate)
- Futures Trading: 0.02% maker / 0.04% taker fees
- 10% reduction with BNB payment for futures

**VIP Level Requirements:**
- **Basic VIP Eligibility:** 30-day Spot volume ≥ $1,000,000 USD OR 30-day Futures volume ≥ $15,000,000 USD OR $200,000 USD OTC + 25 BNB daily average
- **BNB Holding Requirement:** Minimum 25 BNB daily average across all accounts

**Key VIP Levels:**
- **VIP 1:** 30-day Spot volume > $1M USD + 25 BNB
  - Spot: 0.09% maker / 0.10% taker
  - With BNB: 0.0675% maker / 0.075% taker
- **VIP 4:** 30-day volume ≥ $75M USD (reduced from $100M in 2024)
- **VIP 5:** 30-day volume ≥ $150M USD + 1,000 BNB
  - Spot: 0.025% maker / 0.031% taker
- **VIP 9:** Multi-billion USD volume + 5,500 BNB
  - Spot: 0.011% maker (0.008250% with BNB discount)

### 1.2 VIP Sub-Programs

**Four VIP Categories:**
1. **Trader VIP (up to VIP 9):** Based on 30-day spot/futures volume
2. **Holder VIP (up to VIP 4):** For diversified portfolio holders
3. **Investor VIP:** For institutional investors
4. **Borrower VIP (up to VIP 9):** Based on 30-day average net borrowing volume

### 1.3 Special Benefits
- VIP 3+ users: 40% discount on spot and margin trading fees
- Higher withdrawal limits and API rate limits
- Dedicated customer support
- Exclusive event invitations

### 2.2 Kraken

**Fee Structure:**
- **Standard:** 0.16% maker / 0.26% taker (varies by pair)
- **Stablecoin pairs:** 0.20% maker
- **Volume-based tiers:** Fees decrease with 30-day volume

**Special Programs:**
- Super-Volume Rebate Tiers for futures ($250M+ volume)
- Maker rebates on select spot pairs
- Separate volume tracking for spot vs. futures

### 2.3 OKX

**Fee Structure:**

- **VIP 1:** 0.045% maker / 0.05% taker ($100K+ assets)

**Features:**

### 2.4 Bybit

**Fee Structure:**

**Features:**
